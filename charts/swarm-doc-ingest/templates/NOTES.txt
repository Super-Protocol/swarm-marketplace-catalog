This is a component, not a listing: it has no hostname of its own and this chart
has no Ingress. The listing that composes it publishes its own front end.

Every request to /v1 needs `Authorization: Bearer <the key>`, which is why
/v1/health is the probe — it is the one endpoint that does not, because a kubelet
carries no token.

  POST /v1/extract   multipart `file`; `?force_ocr=true`, `?images=true`, `?source_location=`
  GET  /v1/config    the model pins and presets this deployment is running
  GET  /v1/health    503 until the models are loaded, then the queue depth

One document is extracted at a time and the rest queue. That is the memory
measurement, not an unfinished feature: a single document peaks at 2.34 GiB
against the {{ .Values.resources.memory }} limit sized from it. Scale with
`replicaCount`, not with workers — and remember the surge pod in the listing's
`resources.min`.
